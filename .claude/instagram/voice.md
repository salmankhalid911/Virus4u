## My positions

*(draft, edit: these come from how Kickster is positioned)*

1. A printed blank is not a brand. If you want your own product, it has to be
   cut and sewn from your own pattern.
2. You should see a factory before you pay it. Any manufacturer that won't do a
   live video call of the floor is a risk.
3. Never pay for bulk until you have held and approved a physical sample.
4. You don't need 500 pieces to start a real brand. 50 is enough to launch.
5. Payment should go to a registered company's bank account, never to a
   personal account.
